About Nahj

Nahj means “the path”. Ours runs from the classroom to the labour market.

Nahj for Development and Training Solutions is an Egyptian company specialised in vocational training, capacity building, skills development and training consultancy. We build every programme around what employers need, combining theory with hands-on practice so trainees leave with skills they can sell.

Our trainers and consultants write and update the curricula, run fully equipped workshops, and measure what each trainee can do at the end, not just who attended.

Our approach

Eight steps from first day to first income

Each programme moves trainees along the same path, adapted to the trade and to their starting level.

Assess

A short entry test of each trainee's level, experience and goals.

Learn

The essential theory and safety rules, explained simply.

Demonstrate

The trainer shows each technique on real tools and materials.

Practise

Repeated hands-on work under supervision, with constant feedback.

Produce

Real products and tasks in conditions that mirror a workplace.

Evaluate

Practical exams and pre/post tests show what each trainee can do.

Connect

Job fairs, employer interviews, internships, or a business plan.

Follow up

Technical, financial and marketing support until the work stands alone.
